Drag, slide, exitPress on a light, drag it and let go. It slides until something stops it. Bring every light out through the door with its colour and symbol.

Four worlds, four ideasPrism Park, Turquoise Bay, Cloudworks and Starfall Gate: walls, resting rings, barriers that open and close, and doors that wait their turn.
92 verified puzzlesEvery level has been checked by a solver. No timers, no lives, and undo is always free.
Help when you want itHints, the shortest solution and skips are there when you're stuck. Every solve earns all three stars.
Make it yoursUnlock calm looks and rare glowing ones with coins and diamonds earned by playing.
