Glissa Privacy Policy
Last updated: 14 October 2026

Glissa is a puzzle game developed by Nakoneg Studio. You do not need an account to play, and the game never asks for your name, e-mail or other contact details. Your progress is saved only on your device.

## Advertising
The game shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, an approximate location based on your IP address, and how you interact with ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent first; you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & data → Privacy choices. In Turkey the game asks before showing personalized ads; elsewhere you can turn them off at any time in Settings → Privacy & data → Personalized ads. Ads that are not personalized are shown either way. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your phone's settings. Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Reminders
If you allow it, the game schedules a few reminders on your phone (for example, when your daily reward is ready). They are created on the device; nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn them off at any time in your phone's settings.

## Usage statistics
To find out which levels are too hard and how the game is played, the game can send usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase: for example which level was started, won or given up, whether a booster was used, and how coins were earned and spent. They are linked to a random ID for this installation, not to your name, e-mail or advertising identifier, and they are not used for advertising. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland this is off unless you turn it on; elsewhere you can turn it off at any time in Settings → Privacy & data → Usage statistics. Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy

## Purchases
In-app purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We receive confirmation that a purchase happened, never your payment details. "Restore purchases" asks the store which lasting purchases your store account owns.

## Who is responsible
The developer, Okan Üregen (Nakoneg Studio), is responsible for the personal data described here (the data controller). Contact: nakoneg@gmail.com.

## Legal bases
For personalized ads and statistics in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and for personalized ads in Turkey, we rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. For ads that are not personalized, for usage statistics elsewhere and for keeping the game working and safe, we rely on our legitimate interest in offering the game for free and improving it; you can object at any time by turning statistics or personalized ads off in Settings. For purchases we rely on carrying out our agreement with you. This follows the GDPR in the EU and the UK and Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

## Transfers abroad
Google processes the advertising and statistics data, also on servers outside Turkey and the EEA. These transfers are made with the safeguards that data protection law requires, such as standard contractual clauses.

## How long data is kept
Your progress stays on your device until you delete the game or reset your progress. Statistics waiting to be sent stay on the device for at most 7 days. Google Analytics keeps usage statistics for 2 months. Google keeps advertising data under its own policy.

## Your rights
You can ask whether data about you is processed, see it, have it corrected or deleted, object to its use and withdraw your consent. You can also complain to the data protection authority of your country. The game does not know who you are, so you can do most of this yourself: turn statistics off in Settings → Privacy & data → Usage statistics, change your ad choices in Settings → Privacy & data → Privacy choices or Settings → Privacy & data → Personalized ads and reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings. For anything else, write to us.

## Children
The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the game and on the store page, with a new date.
